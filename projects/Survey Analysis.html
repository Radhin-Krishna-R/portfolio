Ground Truth: What 42 Business Owners Taught Me About India's MSME Credit Gap

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to transform raw survey responses into clear, segmentable insights.

Why Survey Analysis Matters

Surveys are one of the most direct ways to capture stakeholder sentiment, customer preferences, and behavioral trends. However, raw survey data is often noisy, multi-dimensional, and difficult to interpret in spreadsheets. Structured visual analysis bridges the gap between collecting responses and making informed decisions.

Objective and Scope

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard built to streamline survey exploration. By organizing responses into intuitive visual views, the dashboard highlights key sentiment drivers, compares patterns across respondent groups, and surfaces outliers that warrant deeper attention.

Dashboard Structure

Executive Summary: High-level KPIs including total respondent count, completion metrics, and headline sentiment or satisfaction scores.

Demographic Breakdown: Distribution of respondents across key segments, helping contextualize results and identify representation gaps.

Question-Level Analysis: Detailed breakdowns of individual survey items, including rating distributions, Likert-scale comparisons, and top-box scores.

Cross-Tabulation & Trends: Side-by-side comparisons showing how attitudes and preferences shift across different respondent cohorts.

Key Features

Dynamic cross-filtering lets users slice the entire dashboard by respondent attributes to isolate specific cohorts.

Clear visual hierarchy pairs summary metrics with granular distributions so both executives and analysts can find answers quickly.

Interactive tooltips surface exact response counts and percentages without cluttering the primary canvas.

Actionable focus makes it easy to spot the highest- and lowest-scoring areas to prioritize follow-up initiatives.

Data Source

Structured survey response dataset prepared and cleaned for multi-dimensional analysis in Tableau.
