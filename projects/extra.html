Macro, Climate & Infrastructure Dashboards

Four interactive Tableau visualizations exploring large-scale public datasets—from 100 years of Kerala monsoon patterns and undersea internet cables to long-form economic deep dives on Brunei and India's 2024 election cycle.

Project Context

This collection focuses on turning complex public-domain datasets into clear visual narratives. The first two dashboards use compact, single-screen layouts for climate time-series and global geospatial mapping. The final two are long-form vertical reports—fitted to the page width inside scrollable viewports so you can explore every section without breaking page flow.
