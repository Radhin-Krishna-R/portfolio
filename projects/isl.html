Indian Super League: Playmakers & Goals

Two interactive Tableau dashboards examining attacking output in the Indian Super League (ISL)—separating the league's primary chance creators from its most clinical goal-scoring trends.

Project Context

Evaluating attacking impact in football requires looking at both sides of the final third: who creates the chances and how goals are actually scored. This two-part Tableau study breaks down the Indian Super League through both lenses.

Dashboard 1 — Playmakers in ISL 2020: Highlights the league's top creative outlets, comparing playmaking contributions, assists, and chance-creation volume across clubs and players.

Dashboard 2 — Goals in the Indian Super League: Tracks scoring distribution, top finishers, and team-level attacking efficiency to show how chance creation translates onto the scoresheet.
