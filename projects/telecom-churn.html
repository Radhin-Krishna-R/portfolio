Telecom Churn Dashboard

A Tableau dashboard for understanding, predicting, and reducing customer churn in telecom.

Why Churn Matters

Customer churn, when subscribers cancel a telecom service, is one of the biggest challenges the industry faces. Its impact goes beyond lost revenue: it damages brand reputation, raises customer acquisition costs, and erodes loyalty. Proactive churn management is essential for long-term sustainability and growth.

Objective and Scope

This project builds a Tableau dashboard that helps telecom businesses understand, predict, and reduce churn. By visualizing complex telecom data, the dashboard uncovers patterns, identifies the main churn drivers, and supports data-driven retention strategies.

Dashboard Structure

Overview: A snapshot of the churn landscape, including KPIs such as churn rate, customer segmentation, and trends over time.

Customer Profile: Individual characteristics such as demographics, usage patterns, and churn propensity scores, showing which segments are most at risk.

Account Analysis: Contract terms, tenure, and service bundles, revealing contract-related churn patterns and how service offerings affect retention.

Product Performance: How products and services influence satisfaction and loyalty, using usage data, feedback, and product-level churn metrics.

Key Features

Interactive filtering lets users segment data by tenure, contract type, or product category.

Granularity control switches between aggregated and detailed views.

Geographic insights use maps to show regional churn patterns for targeted retention efforts.

Actionable recommendations highlight the areas of concern that need attention first.

Data Source

Publicly available telecom sample data from Maven Analytics.
