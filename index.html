About

I am a quantitative researcher with a strong foundation in statistical methods, machine learning, and data visualization. I hold a B.Sc. in Data Science (9.5 CGPA) and am currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Data Science at Vellore Institute of Technology.

My experience spans from evaluating policy programs using causal inference at Naya Neethi Policy & Co. to developing deep learning frameworks (TFT, N-BEATS) for macroeconomic stress testing. I am passionate about translating complex datasets into validated findings and communicating quantitative insights that drive better financial and product decisions.
